# Foyer: Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 14 October 2026*

Foyer is a private, non-commercial household budgeting application. It is used only by the two members of one household to track their own joint bank account. It is not offered to the public, and nobody else can create an account.

## Who is responsible

Foyer is operated by its two users, for their own household. Contact: hadrien.hartstein@gmail.com

## What data Foyer processes

From the joint account the users linked themselves, through Enable Banking's account information service:

- account identifiers (IBAN, account name, account holder names);
- account balances;
- transactions: date, amount, currency, label, counterparty name.

Entered by the users themselves: their own expense entries, categories, income figures, and the last four digits of their own payment cards.

Foyer never sees bank login credentials. Access is authorised by the users directly with their bank.

## Why

The data is used only to sort the household's own expenses, and to work out what each member owes to, or is owed by, the joint account. It is never sold, shared for marketing or used for advertising, and Foyer uses no tracking or analytics.

Because this is a purely personal and household activity, it falls under the household exemption of the GDPR (Article 2(2)(c)). The principles below still apply.

## Who else handles the data

- **Enable Banking Oy** (Finland), a licensed account information service provider, fetches the account data from the bank. Its own privacy policy applies to its processing.
- **A hosting provider in the European Union**, which runs the server Foyer is stored on.

No data is transferred outside the European Union.

## Security

- Read-only access: Foyer cannot initiate payments or move money.
- All connections are encrypted (TLS), and requests to Enable Banking are signed with a private key held only on Foyer's server.
- Both accounts are protected by a password and two-factor authentication.
- Backups are encrypted before they leave the server.
- Only the fields Foyer needs are stored. Raw bank responses are not kept.

## How long data is kept

For as long as the household uses Foyer. When a user asks, or when Foyer is shut down, all stored data and backups are deleted.

## Withdrawing access

Bank access can be revoked at any time from the bank's own app or website. Once revoked, Foyer receives no new data.
